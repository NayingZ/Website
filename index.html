Genetic epidemiology · Childhood cancer

Tracing the inherited roots of childhood cancer.

I study germline genetic susceptibility to childhood and early-onset cancers — using newborn blood spots, population registries and multi-ancestry genomic data to find heritable risk where it has been least well characterized.
