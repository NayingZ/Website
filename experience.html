Clinical Experience

Aug – Sep 2026 Visiting Physician Observer, Medical Genetics
University of California, Los Angeles · Los Angeles, CA
Details
  • Eight weeks of daily medical genetics clinics: general/dysmorphology, metabolic, cancer, prenatal, and skeletal dysplasia.
  • Prepared for each patient by reviewing charts, pedigrees, phenotypes, prior genetic testing, and relevant literature.
  • Built phenotype-driven differentials and refined them through GeneReviews, primary literature, and post-clinic discussions.
  • Presented cases and tested my clinical reasoning with physicians and genetic counselors.
  • Followed how teams moved from phenotype to test selection, variant interpretation, counseling, and management.
  • Focused on how molecular diagnoses changed care, and how clinicians translated uncertainty into decisions families could act on.
  • This experience brought my genomics research back to the bedside. I saw firsthand how a genetic diagnosis can guide counseling, surveillance, treatment, and secondary prevention, strengthening my goal to bring my genetic knowledge back to patient care and make risk actionable for families.

Bristol Myers Squibb, Global Biometrics & Data Sciences · Seattle, WA
Details
  • Three-month biostatistics internship supporting luspatercept development in alpha-thalassemia and low-risk myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS), through clinical trial and real-world data evidence.
